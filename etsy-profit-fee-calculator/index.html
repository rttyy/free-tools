About this free tool

This calculator helps Etsy sellers, handmade crafters, and digital creators accurately calculate platform fees and real net margins. Enter your production costs and retail price to see exact deductions and stress-test sale discounts. Use the free web tool anytime, or grab the offline pack to manage your catalog locally.

How to use it

  1. Enter your base product costs, including materials, packaging, and hourly labor time.
  2. Input your retail price and the shipping amount charged to the buyer.
  3. Select your applicable fee settings, such as payment processing region and Offsite Ads rate.
  4. Review your net profit per unit and adjust the discount slider to find your exact break-even price.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Etsy take per sale in total?

For US sellers, Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the combined item and shipping cost, and a payment processing fee of 3% plus $0.25. In total, standard platform fees usually average between 9.5% and 12% of the order total. If an order comes through Etsy's Offsite Ads program, an additional 12% to 15% fee applies.

Does the calculator account for Etsy Offsite Ads fees?

Yes. You can toggle Offsite Ads on or off and select either the 15% rate (standard for shops under $10,000 USD in trailing 12-month sales) or the 12% rate (for shops over $10,000 USD). This shows you whether an advertised order remains profitable at your current price point.

How does the discount slider calculate break-even pricing?

The break-even slider recalculates your net take-home profit as you adjust your promo percentage from 0% to 50%. It identifies the exact discount threshold where net profit drops to zero after all listing fees, transaction cuts, payment processing, and cost of goods sold are subtracted.
