Privacy policy

Free Interactive Tools · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Summary

The tools on this site run entirely in your browser. This site does not ask for an account, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and does not collect or sell personal data.

Data you enter in the tools

What you type into a tool stays on your device. Some tools save your inputs in your browser's local storage so you can find them again later. That data is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Purchases

Optional offline packs are sold through Gumroad. If you buy one, Gumroad processes your payment and your details under its own privacy policy. We receive only what Gumroad shares with sellers to deliver the product.

Pinterest

We use the Pinterest API only to publish our own Pins about these tools to our own Pinterest account. This integration does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the seller through the Gumroad product pages linked from each tool.
