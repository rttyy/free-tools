About this free tool

The Micro-Zone Room Reset Studio helps neurodivergent individuals and overwhelmed parents break cluttered spaces into tiny, approachable steps. By matching cleanup actions directly to your current energy level, the app bypasses executive freeze without forcing rigid routines. Use the free online version in your browser or unlock the standalone offline pack for physical printables and local exports.

How to use it

  1. Select your target micro-zone (e.g., desk surface, bedside table, or entryway path).
  2. Set your current energy level to generate low-, medium-, or high-intensity steps.
  3. Follow the guided micro-actions accompanied by subtle Web Audio chimes or ambient noise.
  4. Export your routine to Markdown/JSON or print a physical cue sheet to keep near your space.

Questions this tool answers

Given my current low energy (fuzzy/exhausted) and only 10 minutes before I have to leave, which micro-zones in my living room can I clear right now to make the biggest visual difference without triggering panic?

You enter: Room type (e.g., Living Room) · Current energy level (scale 1 to 5, e.g., 2 = low/sluggish) · Available time budget (e.g., 10 minutes) · Visual clutter state of zones (e.g., Coffee Table: heavy clutter, Couch: medium clutter, Floor: high clutter).
You get: An ordered, achievable micro-sequence of 1-3 specific micro-actions sized to fit exactly 10 minutes, with per-zone countdown timers and projected clutter reduction percentage.

How much total time and mental stamina would it take to reset my entire kitchen if I break it down into tiny bite-sized micro-zones, and what order should I tackle them in to avoid getting stuck halfway?

You enter: Room type (Kitchen) · List of zones present and their current clutter rating (Sink zone: 5/5, Prep counter: 4/5, Stovetop: 3/5, Trash/recycling: 5/5) · Target energy pace (e.g., 3 = steady moderate pace).
You get: Calculated total estimated reset duration, optimized friction-ranked route (e.g., bin empty first to create disposal capacity, then soak, then clear surfaces), and expected stamina drain profile.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the free web page and the paid Gumroad product?

The free web page lets you generate and run micro-zone routines online. The $8 paid product is a single, downloadable HTML file that works 100% offline with no internet connection required, and it unlocks routine exports (JSON/Markdown) alongside physical printable cue sheets.

Do I need to install any software or create an account?

No. The offline app is a single HTML file that opens directly in any standard web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). There are no user accounts, logins, or backend servers involved.

How does the tool adapt to low energy levels?

Instead of prompting you to do deep cleaning, selecting a low energy level scales down the tasks to essential, low-friction micro-actions—such as removing obvious trash or clearing a single surface—preventing executive overload.

Is my session history and data kept private?

Yes, entirely. All your cleared zones, day streaks, and custom settings are stored locally on your device in your browser's LocalStorage. No data ever leaves your computer.
