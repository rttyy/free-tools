About this free tool

Pendulum Wave is a free interactive acoustics sandbox that transforms physical harmonic pendulum oscillations into polyrhythmic bell patterns and generative soundscapes. Designed for ambient producers, sound designers, and physics enthusiasts, it offers a real-time visual and auditory demonstration of phase relationships and microtonal tuning systems.

How to use it

  1. Select your tuning system, such as Just Intonation or overtone series presets.
  2. Adjust the pendulum count, frequency spread, and dampening parameters to shape the phase cycle.
  3. Engage visualizer modes to view kinetic trajectories and wave formations.
  4. Hit the record button to capture the generative acoustic output directly into a lossless WAV file.
