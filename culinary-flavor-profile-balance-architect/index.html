About this free tool

The Culinary Flavor Profile & Balance Architect is a practical tool designed to help home cooks transition from strictly following recipes to cooking by ear. It allows you to visually map and diagnose off-tasting dishes across six core taste axes directly in your browser. Whether your meal is too sour or lacking depth, the tool helps you make confident adjustments before adding ingredients to the pot.

How to use it

  1. Select your dish's current intensity across Salt, Acid, Fat, Sweet, Heat, and Umami.
  2. Review the interactive SVG radar chart to spot missing or overpowering flavor axes.
  3. Hover over candidate ingredients to preview ghost-line impacts on your flavor profile.
  4. Track your adjustments side-by-side using the A/B Taste Evolution Comparator.
  5. Export your complete recipe dossier or print a kitchen-ready fix sheet for reference.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an active internet connection to use the tool?

The online web app is free to access anytime with an internet connection. The paid $9 Gumroad pack includes a self-contained offline HTML file that runs entirely in your web browser without needing internet or software installation.

How do live ingredient ghost lines work?

When you hover over potential fixes like lemon juice or soy sauce, the tool overlays a translucent secondary line on your radar chart. This lets you visually preview how an ingredient shifts your 6-axis balance before committing it to the pot.

Is this tool suitable for beginner cooks?

Yes. It is designed specifically for home cooks who know basic steps but struggle when a dish tastes flat, overly salty, or too acidic. It translates abstract taste balancing into concrete visual feedback.

What file formats can I export my recipe fixes to?

You can export your flavor logs and recipe dossiers as CSV, JSON, formatted text dossiers, or print kitchen-ready fix sheets directly from your browser.
