About this free tool
Pet Clicker Training & Micro-Shaping Studio is a browser-based utility that provides zero-latency acoustic markers, real-time behavioral decision rules, and automated curriculum generation for animal training. It helps dog and cat trainers track trial success rates and break down complex behaviors into manageable micro-steps. You can use the free web version directly in your browser or purchase the $9 offline studio pack for internet-free access.


How to use it
  1. Select or generate a curriculum micro-step by setting distance, duration, and distraction parameters.
  2. Trigger the zero-latency Web Audio clicker or whistle acoustic marker when your pet performs the target behavior.
  3. Log each trial response as a success or failure to calculate real-time success percentages.
  4. Follow the live behavioral decision directives to advance, hold, or split your training criteria.


Questions this tool answers
How do I break down my pet's target trick into granular micro-shaping criteria steps tailored to their species and temperament, and what exact readiness threshold must they hit before I raise criteria?
You enter: Pet species (dog, cat, bird/small pet) · Target behavior/trick (e.g., ring desk bell, target stick touch, roll over) · Pet energy level (low/hesitant, balanced, high/hyperactive) · Primary reinforcer type (kibble, high-value meat, lickable paste, toy/play).
You get: An ordered sequence of 5 micro-shaping criteria steps with physical distance/duration metrics, prompt-fading instructions, and the target advancement threshold (e.g., 80% success across 5 consecutive trials).
During an active training session, my pet just performed 7 successes and 3 misses over 10 repetitions; should I raise the criterion, stick at the current level, split the step into an easier slice, or end the session?
You enter: Current step number and criteria definition · Number of successes in current session (e.g., 7) · Number of misses/refusals in current session (e.g., 3) · Current consecutive miss streak (e.g., 2) · Session elapsed duration (e.g., 180 seconds).
You get: A definitive behavioral verdict (STICK, SPLIT, RAISE, or JACKPOT & END) with click rate (clicks per minute) and actionable handler directives to prevent frustration or extinction.


Frequently asked questions
Why use Web Audio synthetic markers instead of playing pre-recorded MP3 clicker sounds?
Pre-recorded audio files often introduce subtle playback latency depending on browser and mobile hardware performance. Web Audio generates synthetic acoustic tones directly via code, eliminating audio lag so your marker timing matches the exact moment of behavior.
What is the difference between the free web page and the paid Gumroad product?
The free web version runs fully featured inside any modern browser while connected online. The $9 Gumroad paid product is a standalone offline HTML/JS bundle that you can save locally to run without internet access, plus print-formatted session reporting utilities.
How does the behavioral decision engine decide when to advance criteria?
The decision engine uses standard behavioral shaping mechanics, tracking your success rate over a rolling block of trials (typically 10 reps). An 80%+ success rate triggers a prompt to advance criteria, while a rate below 60% prompts you to lower criteria or split the step.
Does this tool require installing software or creating a user account?
No. The application runs entirely client-side in your web browser with zero software installation, zero account sign-ups, and no telemetry tracking. All session data stays stored locally in your browser or can be exported via CSV and JSON.
