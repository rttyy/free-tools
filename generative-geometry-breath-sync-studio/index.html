About this free tool

Generative Sacred Geometry & Breath Sync Studio is a browser-based visual pacer designed for meditation practitioners, yoga teachers, and breathwork facilitators. It combines customizable sacred geometry visualizers with breath-synchronized audio sweeps and physiological tracking metrics. The core tool is free to use online, with an offline single-file studio pack available for download.

How to use it

  1. Select a visual anchor (Sacred Mandala, Spirograph, Lotus, or Wavefront) and configure your preferred color palette.
  2. Enter your desired breathing pattern (Inhale, Inhale Hold, Exhale, Exhale Hold) or reverse-calculate intervals using target breaths per minute.
  3. Enable breath-synchronized binaural beats and harmonic overtone sweeps to guide your pacing through sound.
  4. Track real-time session indicators such as estimated HRV potential, vagal engagement, and export your session data to JSON or CSV.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the free web page and the $9 Gumroad product?

The free web page provides full access to the interactive geometry visualizers, audio generator, and pacing controls directly in your browser. The $9 Gumroad product is a standalone, single-file HTML package that runs entirely offline with no internet connection required, and it permanently unlocks local JSON/CSV session logging for facilitators and personal archives.

How does the reverse-solve breath timing feature work?

Instead of forcing you to mentally divide seconds, the studio lets you input your target breathing rate (such as 5.5 breaths per minute for resonance) alongside your preferred ratio (such as 1:2 inhale-to-exhale). The engine instantly calculates the exact millisecond values for each phase of the breathing cycle.

Do I need headphones to use the audio features?

Headphones or stereo earbuds are recommended if you want the full benefit of binaural beat frequencies, as they rely on presenting slightly different frequencies to each ear. However, the harmonic overtone sweeps and visual breathing cues work effectively over standard external speakers or in complete silence.

How are physiological indicators like HRV potential and vagal brake engagement calculated?

These indicators are algorithmic estimates based on peer-reviewed autonomic research correlating respiratory rate, prolonged exhalation ratios, and breath hold durations with parasympathetic activation. They provide comparative benchmarks for your sessions rather than direct clinical ECG or optical sensor measurements.
