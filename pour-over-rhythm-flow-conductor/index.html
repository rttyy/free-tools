Questions Answered & Technical Method

1. How is the pulse schedule computed?

User Inputs: Coffee Dose ($D$), Water Ratio ($R$), Bloom Mass ($W_b$), Bloom Pour/Rest Times, Pulse Count ($N$), Pulse Durations. Outputs: Total Water ($W_{total} = D \times R$), Pulse Volume ($W_p = (W_{total} - W_b) / N$), and Target Flow Rates ($Q = \text{Volume} / \text{Time}$).

Worked Example: 15.0g dose @ 1:16 ratio = 240.0g total water. 45.0g bloom leaves 195.0g remaining. 3 pulses = 65.0g per pulse. 13s pour = 5.0 g/s flow rate.

About this free tool

The Pour-Over Coffee Flow & Rhythm Conductor is an interactive tool designed for coffee enthusiasts and home baristas to dial in pour speeds, pulse intervals, and cup extraction. The free web version guides your daily brews with real-time visual and audio flow pacing. The $9 offline pack provides full offline desktop functionality, CSV/JSON recipe data export, and printable 4x6 recipe cards.

How to use it

  1. Input your coffee dose (g), target water ratio, and preferred pulse pour schedule.
  2. Follow real-time visual and audio metronome cues to maintain your target flow rate (g/s).
  3. Log live scale weights to monitor flow variance and brewing schedule deviations.
  4. Adjust grind size offsets to estimate changes in extraction yield (%) and beverage TDS (%).
  5. Export recipe data to JSON/CSV or print a 4x6 recipe card for your coffee setup.

Frequently asked questions

Why does flow rate (g/s) matter for pour-over coffee?

Flow rate dictates the level of agitation in the coffee bed and influences contact time. A erratic flow rate causes uncontrolled turbulence and uneven extraction, whereas a steady flow rate delivers predictable clarity and sweetness.

What is included in the $9 paid offline pack versus the free web page?

The web tool is completely free to use online in any browser. The $9 paid pack works offline without internet, enables raw data export to JSON/CSV for brewing logs, and includes a print-ready 4x6 recipe card generator.

How does the tool calculate estimated extraction yield and TDS?

It uses standard brew control formulas based on coffee dose, total water weight, beverage retention, and grind offset parameters. While an optical refractometer provides precise physical TDS measurements, this calculation offers a quick mathematical baseline for recipe adjustments.

Do I need a smart Bluetooth scale to use this conductor?

No smart scale is required. You can use any digital kitchen scale to read weights while following the visual and audio metronome prompts on screen to pace your pour.
