Frequently asked questions

My LTFT and STFT values are off and don't make sense. Why?

Usually, this happens because fuel trims are additive. If your LTFT is +20% and STFT is -5%, your total trim is +15%. If the numbers are erratic, you may have a 'biased' O2 sensor or an intermittent vacuum leak that only opens when the engine moves under torque.

Why won't my engine rotate?

If the engine physically won't turn, this is a mechanical failure rather than a tuning issue. Check for a seized starter, a dead battery (solenoid click), or 'hydrolock' where liquid has filled a cylinder. Fuel trim diagnostics require a running engine to provide data.

What possible causes for the 'check engine' light can I check for myself?

The most common DIY checks include a loose gas cap, dirty Mass Airflow (MAF) sensors, or cracked vacuum lines. Using fuel trim data helps you narrow these down to the specific system before you start cleaning or replacing parts.
