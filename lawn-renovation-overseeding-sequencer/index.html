About this free tool

This tool helps lawn hobbyists and homeowners plan complex renovations without the risk of timing errors or material waste. It sequences your chemical applications, calculates precise soil volumes, and generates a printable execution schedule.

How to use it

  1. Enter your target seeding date and the herbicides you've recently applied.
  2. Input your lawn's square footage and desired topdressing depth to calculate material volume.
  3. Adjust the irrigation settings based on your specific soil type and sunlight exposure.
  4. Export the generated timeline to CSV or print it as a step-by-step run-sheet.

Frequently asked questions

How do I fix the worst lawn on the street?

Start with a 'blank slate' approach: use a non-selective herbicide to kill weeds, core aerate to relieve compaction, and use this sequencer to time your new seed application. Focusing on soil health and proper seed-to-soil contact is more effective than patchy repairs.

Will leveling uneven lawns with extra sand kill future lawns?

Sand itself won't kill grass, but applying too much at once smothers the crown and prevents photosynthesis. Use the tool to keep your applications under 0.5 inches per pass to ensure the existing turf can grow through the new layer.

What can I plant in dandelion graves?

Once dandelions are removed, you have a void of bare soil. Scuff the soil surface, add a pinch of high-quality matching seed, and use the sequencer to track the specific irrigation needs for those 'spot-seeded' areas to ensure they fill in before new weeds take hold.
