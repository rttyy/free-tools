About this free tool

The Indoor Sun Path & Shadow Mapping Studio helps houseplant owners and interior designers simulate solar angles and light intensity inside any room. The interactive web tool is completely free to use in your browser with no account setup required. For users needing local storage and data exports, an offline desktop pack is available for $9.

How to use it

  1. Enter your room dimensions, ceiling height, latitude, and window orientation.
  2. Place up to 4 virtual plant shelves or candidate furniture spots inside the grid.
  3. Scrub through the 365-day timeline to watch real-time shadow movement and sun penetration.
  4. Review daily direct sun hours to match your plants with their ideal light requirements.

Questions this tool answers

How many hours of direct sunlight will a plant on a shelf 1.8 meters back from a South-facing window get in June versus December?

You enter: Location latitude (e.g. 40.7°N New York) · Window orientation (180° South) · Window dimensions (1.8m wide, 1.6m high, sill height 0.8m) · Plant spot location (X=2.0m centered, Y=1.8m depth into room, Z=0.9m shelf height).
You get: Daily direct sunlight duration in hours on June 21 vs. December 21, exact enter/exit times, and peak illumination level at that spot.

Is my current indoor plant location bright enough for a light-hungry plant like a Fiddle Leaf Fig, or will I need to relocate it during winter?

You enter: Room dimensions and window orientation · Plant spot distance from window · Current season/month.
You get: Integrated daily Lux-Hours (kLux-hours/day) for each month of the year, target plant light requirement match percentage, and a seasonal relocation recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free online web page differ from the $9 paid pack?

The interactive web version is completely free for mapping rooms directly in your web browser. The $9 paid pack contains self-contained HTML/JS files that run locally without an internet connection, along with CSV and JSON data export tools for 365-day hourly schedules.

Do I need to install software or set up an account to use this?

No. The core tool runs on standard web technologies inside modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari without any installations or sign-ups.

How accurate are the calculated sun angles and shadows?

The studio uses standard astronomical algorithms for solar position (azimuth and elevation) combined with geometric ray-tracing based on your room dimensions. External obstructions like trees or neighboring buildings are not calculated automatically and should be estimated.

Can this tool be used for interior design projects other than houseplants?

Yes. Interior designers use it to check screen glare on desks, identify areas prone to floor fading, and plan room layouts based on seasonal daylight availability.
