About this free tool

This tool helps you visualize and plan your temperature blanket using real-world data and precise yarn math. While the basic version is free online, the Offline Pack provides a private, standalone file with full CSV import/export and printable guides.

How to use it

  1. Define your temperature ranges and assign your yarn colors.
  2. Import historical weather data via CSV or enter it manually to see a 365-day preview.
  3. Input your swatch measurements to calculate the exact number of skeins needed per color.
  4. Export your data to JSON/CSV or print the 12-month stitch guide to track your daily progress offline.

Questions this tool answers

How should I divide my temperature ranges so that my 8 colors are evenly distributed across the year without one color dominating the whole blanket?

You enter: Local historical average temperatures for their location · The number of yarn colors they want to use (e.g., 8).
You get: A visualization of the blanket based on historical data and a breakdown of what percentage of the year each color will occupy.

How much yarn of each color do I actually need to buy, and how long will the finished blanket be if I use a specific stitch?

You enter: Yarn weight/WPI (Wraps Per Inch) · Stitch height (mm or inches) · Stitch width (planned blanket width) · Historical or simulated daily temperatures.
You get: Total yardage or grams required per color and the final physical dimensions (Length x Width) of the project.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool require an internet connection to work?

The Gumroad Offline Pack is a single HTML file that runs entirely on your local computer. Once downloaded, you do not need an internet connection to use any of its features, ensuring your data remains private.

Can I import data from my own weather station?

Yes. The planner supports CSV imports. As long as your data has a date and a temperature column, you can map it to the tool to generate your specific local gradient.

How does the yarn calculator work?

You input the length of yarn used for a single stitch or row from your gauge swatch. The tool then multiplies this by the frequency of each temperature range to give you the total meterage and number of skeins required.

Is this a subscription service?

No. This is a one-time purchase of $8 for the offline tool. You own the file and can use it for as many projects and as many years as you like without further fees.
