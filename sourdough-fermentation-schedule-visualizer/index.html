About this free tool

The Sourdough & Fermentation Schedule Visualizer is a free web tool built for home bakers who want reliable bread timing without over-proofing. It combines a dynamic baker's percentage calculator with temperature-based fermentation modeling so you can plan schedules backward from your target bake time.

How to use it

  1. Enter your target flour weight, hydration, and starter percentage to generate your ingredient bench sheet.
  2. Set your ambient kitchen temperature and target bake time to let the engine project your fermentation timeline.
  3. Check the container canvas to preview expected dough volume expansion and porosity.
  4. Export your custom schedule to JSON or print an offline bench sheet to track your bake by hand.

Questions this tool answers

My kitchen is 20°C (68°F) in winter instead of 26°C (78°F) in summer. If I mix my dough at 9:00 AM with 20% starter, when will bulk fermentation actually peak, and at what clock times should I perform my 4 stretch-and-folds?

You enter: Mix start time (09:00 AM) · Ambient room temperature (20°C) · Dough initial water temperature / mixed dough temperature (21°C) · Starter inoculation percentage (20% of total flour) · Planned number of stretch-and-fold sets (4 sets, spaced every 30 minutes at start).
You get: Exact predicted clock times for each stretch-and-fold set, the estimated peak bulk fermentation end time (and target volume increase percentage before shaping), and the total elapsed hours of bulk proofing.

I want to bake a sourdough loaf on Sunday at 8:00 AM using an overnight refrigerator cold retard. What time on Saturday must I mix my dough and when must it go into the fridge so it does not over-proof?

You enter: Target bake day and time (Sunday 08:00 AM) · Refrigerator temperature (4°C) · Ambient kitchen temperature on Saturday (22°C) · Starter inoculation (20%) · Planned cold retard duration (e.g. 14 hours).
You get: A backward-calculated schedule showing Saturday mix time, Saturday bulk end/shaping time, Saturday fridge entry time, and Sunday preheat/bake time with estimated total proofing progression percentage.

Frequently asked questions

How does the tool estimate bulk fermentation duration?

The algorithm applies basic thermal kinetics derived from standard sourdough fermentation rates between 18°C and 28°C. It adjusts the projected duration based on starter inoculation percentage, dough hydration, and expected ambient temperature drift in your kitchen.

What is the difference between the free web page and the $9 Gumroad pack?

The online visualizer is completely free to use in any modern web browser. The $9 Gumroad offline pack provides a standalone, self-hosted build that runs without an internet connection, along with printable PDF bench sheet templates and JSON recipe export presets.

Can I use this for pizza dough or commercial yeast recipes?

Yes. While tuned primarily for wild sourdough starter kinetics, you can adjust starter percentages and hydration settings to fit direct yeasted doughs or poolish-based pizza dough schedules.

Why does the tool schedule backward from the bake time?

Working backward prevents the common issue of dough peaking at inconvenient hours, such as 2:00 AM. By fixing your final oven time first, the visualizer tells you precisely when to build your levain, mix dough, and begin cold retarding.
